What changed and how to read this comparison

Candidate __VERSION__ smooths the curves of 2/3/5/6/8/9 and gives figures a slightly lighter stroke. Quotes, commas, dots, and dashes make quieter marks; quotation marks keep their raised position. All numeric alternates follow the updated figures. Light gains a little substance, while Medium gains a little counter room. Regular, SemiBold, and Bold keep their letter drawings. These are static weight refinements, applied at every size; there is no automatic optical-size switching.

Every character advance, the established letter-pair rhythm, word space, heights, contrast, and italic angle match __BASELINE__. Only pairs involving punctuation or figures are remeasured. Symbols and icons keep their drawings. The earlier reading words, real fi/fl joins, accents, and cursor stops carry forward. The two figure rows show proportional defaults and optional tabular figures separately.

Compare 20px at 1.4 and 1.5 line height, then 13/15/17px at Regular and Medium. Use Full reading width for longer lines. The sample can help identify differences; sustained reading and the readability guidance still call for feedback from readers.

Material's body-text guidance suggests around 1.5 line height. Google's web-font guide covers weight/style mapping and font loading. This page embeds the real fonts and makes no network font requests.
