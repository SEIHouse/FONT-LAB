What changed and how to read this comparison

Candidate __VERSION__ preserves every __BASELINE__ glyph contour and advance, including the refined lowercase and handwriting-style italics. Focused pair adjustments measure the central lowercase space for each weight and style, limit the influence of open edges, and protect the closest ink from crowding. rn/rm keep extra room for distinction. Joined fi/fl now receive pair spacing beside their neighbors; their internal shapes remain unchanged. Accent derivatives share their base letter’s pair classes. Word space, heights, weights, contrast, and raised quotes stay the same.

Compare 20px at 1.4 and 1.5 line height, then 13/15/17px at Regular and Medium. Use Full reading width for longer lines. The sample can help identify differences; sustained reading and the readability guidance still call for feedback from readers.
