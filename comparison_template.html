What changed and how to read this comparison

Candidate __VERSION__ keeps the 0.27 heights, weights, contrast, squared caps, raised quotes, and handwriting-style italic construction. Its upright a has a smaller bowl, e has an open exit, and c/s have more flowing terminals. The shared e/c/s curves also refine the italics. The i gets slightly more side space. Pair spacing is recalculated for every style.

Compare 20px at 1.4 and 1.5 line height, then 13/15/17px at Regular and Medium. Use Full reading width for longer lines. The sample can help identify differences; sustained reading and the readability guidance still call for feedback from readers.

Material's body-text guidance suggests around 1.5 line height. Google's web-font guide covers weight/style mapping and font loading. This page embeds the real fonts and makes no network font requests.
