What is different

Why it reads as more professional

Strokes with life

Upright strokes are full, horizontal ones a little lighter, and curves pass smoothly between the two, so text has an even, calm colour.

Clean joins

Arches and bowls thin as they meet a stem. The dark knots where n, m, b and d used to clot are gone.

Reading weight

Regular now carries a proper text weight, close to Google Noto Sans. The earlier lightness lives on as the Light.

Fitted spacing

Each letter is spaced by its shape, and awkward pairs such as To, AV and Yo are kerned automatically.

A family

Light, Regular, Medium and Bold come from the same drawing rules, so they stay related.

Room to write

Full keyboard symbols and accented letters for most European languages, up from 94 characters.

True italics

A real italic for thoughts, voices and emphasis

The lantern had gone out an hour ago. Don’t move, Kaien told himself, not yet — he is still listening. Somewhere below, water dripped into the dark. “You hear that?” she whispered. Of course I hear it. He said nothing, and the silence answered for him.

Italic letters are drawn, not tilted. The a is single-storey, n, m, h, u, i, a and d leave in a small exit stroke toward the next letter, arches branch lower, and f drops below the line. The two-storey g, hooked l and curved y stay, so it is still recognisably SEIHouse. All four weights keep their upright partners’ spacing logic, and the browser never has to fake a slant.
