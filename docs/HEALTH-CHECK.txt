SEIReader 0.32 health check
Checked with FontBakery 1.1.0 (Google's font checker), all 10 full OTF files.

  Universal offline: 675 passed, 0 failed, 0 warnings  (457 skipped, 30 info)
  OpenType checks:   257 passed, 0 failed, 0 warnings  (201 did not apply)

  Universal was run with --skip-network. Online-only checks were not run for 0.32.
  The previous full 0.27 run returned 10 connection failures for fontbakery_version and
  10 service errors for fontdata_namecheck. Those online checks remain unverified.

  Phase 2 shaping and all 30 web subsets verified after the final rebuild.
  All 10 preserved 0.31 full WOFF2 files and 30 subsets match their SHA-256 manifests,
  and were also compared byte-for-byte with commit 1c0bbc7dc2f6e9a8f07f5c056c732ddac03cb924.
  verify_texture.py passes across all 10 candidate styles:
    - All 295 advances and all nominal design settings except version match 0.31.
    - 85 intended mark/figure contours change in Regular/SemiBold/Bold and their italics.
      Light/Medium also change 119 letter/derivative contours (204 total per style).
    - Unrelated contours/bearings, symbols/icons, private aliases, compact ordinal marks,
      vertical metrics, contrast, and italic angle are preserved.
    - Letter-pair spacing and letter-only word shaping exactly match 0.31. Only pairs
      involving marks/figures change: 940-947 per upright and 772-854 per italic style.
    - The built n has +5.13%/+4.98% ink in Light/LightItalic and -2.58%/-2.54% in
      Medium/MediumItalic. Other weights' letter drawings are identical to 0.31.
    - Letter counters, 0/6/8/9 counters and their tabular/small variants, tall-letter
      alignment, and raised quotes pass. Weight order remains Light < Regular < Medium
      < SemiBold < Bold, upright and italic.
    - Alignment zones match the baseline; standard stems follow the calibrated letter
      strokes and stem hints remain in every style.
    - Disabling kern preserves all tested glyph choices, advances, and offsets.
    - Accent classes inherit base spacing. All changed mark/figure pairs, their accented
      derivatives, earlier words, broader words, and dialogue cases have no adjacent
      outline intersections. Word samples are checked with joins both enabled and disabled.
    - Each subset's local encoded text shapes exactly like the full font.
    - Every generated Lab pair agrees with the corresponding finished font.
  Historical verify_lowercase.py old/0.31 and verify_spacing.py old/0.31 also pass,
  preserving their earlier scope audits against 0.28 and 0.30 respectively.

  The reference preview embeds 24 font faces. Its script syntax and embedded font bytes
  were checked locally. Fixed 13/15/17px Light/Regular/Medium samples include upright,
  real italic, dialogue, decimals, proportional/tabular figures, fractions, accents, and
  joins on/off. Native Windows WPF grayscale proofs were inspected at 96 dpi in Day
  and Night, both at small sizes and all five weights at 20px; see docs/proofs/0.32/,
  its family/ and rhythm/ directories. These use HarfBuzz-shaped glyph indices
  and the real candidate/baseline outlines, with no font installation or simulated bold.
  The in-app browser automation could not connect. Physical phone/browser checks are
  deferred at the user's request; no device or sustained-reading comfort pass is claimed.
