# SEIReader preliminary name check

Research date: **2026-10-03**. Proposed owner, as supplied by the project owner:
**SEIHouse Productions LLC, Ohio**. Product: the SEIReader font family and its
use in SEIHouse reading applications. Company ownership and formation were not
independently verified.

**Historical finding for SEIReader: the name is not cleared.** Exact-name searches below found no matching
trademark record in the accessible USPTO and WIPO searches, but **SEEREADER is a
live US registration for online literacy software**. This is a material close-name
candidate. EUIPO and Fontspring access gaps also remain. Obtain a trademark
attorney's assessment before a public launch or filing under SEIReader.

This is a dated preliminary screening report, not a legal opinion or an exhaustive
clearance search. A missing result is limited to the database, query, and date.

## Quick alternative-name screen: SEIHouse Sans

On **2026-10-03**, a short public-web search for `SEIHouse Sans`, `SEI House Sans`,
`SEIHouseSans`, and `SEIHouse` with font/typeface/trademark terms found no identified
third-party font using the proposed full name. Domain-restricted searches covering
Google Fonts, MyFonts, Adobe Fonts, Fontspring, GitHub, USPTO, EUIPO, and WIPO also
returned no indexed matching listing. General SEIHouse results included the
[existing SEIHouse Productions site](https://senseidukes.com/seihouse-productions).
This was a quick indexed-web screen, not new native registry searches or a guarantee
of availability. **SEIHouse Sans is a reasonable candidate on this limited evidence.**
The owner approved proceeding with SEIHouse Sans and ecosystem licensing; the
current 0.36 font metadata uses that name. The SEIReader findings below
remain a record of the previous name, not findings about SEIHouse Sans.

## Search method and scope

Checked `SEIReader` / `SeiReader` / `SEIREADER`, `SEI Reader`, `SEI-Reader`, and
`SEIRead`; expanded the US search to names containing `seiread`, combinations of
`sei` and `reader`, and sound-alike candidates using `see`, `say`, and `read*`.
Capitalization alone was not treated as a different name. These expansions do
not constitute a complete phonetic or linguistic search; the intended pronunciation
of SEIReader has not been supplied.

Public browser interfaces were used for database searches where accessible.
Search-engine queries supplemented them, including quoted exact/spaced/hyphenated
names restricted to each named catalog and registry domain. No class, territory,
or live-only filter was applied to the exact USPTO/WIPO searches. Search-engine
index absence was never counted as a database clearance result.

### Exact identifiers already used in software

Google/Android ExoPlayer code uses the Java class name `SeiReader`; see
[Google's Android source](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/prebuilts/fullsdk/sources/+/refs/heads/androidx-constraintlayout-release/android-35/com/google/android/exoplayer2/extractor/ts/H265Reader.java).
Fraunhofer's HEVC reference implementation uses C++ `SEIReader`; see its
[published SEIread.cpp source](https://hevc.hhi.fraunhofer.de/HM-doc/_s_e_iread_8cpp_source.html).
These are supplemental-enhancement-information video parser identifiers. They
create search noise and naming overlap, but are not evidence by themselves of
trademark use for a typeface or a competing font product.

## Risk assessment and remaining clearance work

**Preliminary assessment:** no exact font-family collision identified in the
accessible searches; **material unresolved trademark risk** from SEEREADER,
additional SEI candidates, and incomplete EU coverage. Do not label the name
"available," "unique," "registered," or "low risk" on this evidence. A license
choice or an OFL Reserved Font Name does not clear third-party trademark rights.

An attorney should:

1. Evaluate SEEREADER's live US registration, actual commercial use, pronunciation,
   related goods, channels, and international records against the proposed font
   and reading-app branding. Determine whether renaming or another resolution is needed.
2. Complete EUIPO/TMview searches, including EU member-state records; validate
   WIPO candidate records in their home offices and search other intended markets.
3. Expand phonetic, spelling, translation, prefix, and design-mark searches;
   investigate the SEI software and education marks and the Reader catalog names.
4. Check US common-law use, state marks and business records (including Ohio),
   domain names, and relevant app/product markets. The LLC name does not establish
   font-name clearance. These checks were not completed in this screening.
5. Advise on filing basis, first-use evidence, owner/assignment records, and goods
   descriptions. Class 9 is a starting point for downloadable font/software goods;
   consider actual services in classes 42/41 as appropriate. Do not limit clearance
   to those class numbers or assume every software product is legally related.
6. Approve any trademark statement for name ID 7. No SEIReader registration was
   verified; do not use the registered-trademark symbol for SEIReader on this basis.

The [USPTO comprehensive-search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks)
explains federal, state, common-law, and international scope. Its
[likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)
explains why similar marks and related goods matter even without an exact match.
